## How I work

- Something real every day: code, tests, reproducing a bug, reading a module, or reviewing. At least an hour on normal days.
- I read CONTRIBUTING and the AI policy before I touch a project's code.
- I write my own code and can explain every line of every PR I open.
- I reply to review comments within 2 days.
- I ask before changing how something behaves.

## Why I keep this

Writing something down every day keeps me consistent, and it makes it easy to look back and see what I've actually done and learned.
